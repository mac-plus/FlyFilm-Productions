About Us

Cinema Is Our
Language

Founded in 2015, APEX FILMS is an independent production house built on one belief: that great film has the power to shift culture. We're a lean, senior-heavy team — directors, DPs, editors, and producers who have worked with major brands and independent artists alike.

We don't do average. Every frame is intentional. Every cut is earned. We're drawn to projects that challenge convention and demand something more.

  • London-based, world-ready crew
  • 4K–8K cinema-grade camera packages
  • In-house DI & color lab
  • Network of international co-production partners
